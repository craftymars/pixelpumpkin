
# 🎃 PixelPumpkin

### A Cozy Halloween-Inspired Browser Mini-Game

Grow your very own pixel-art pumpkin, one little step at a time! 🌱

**PixelPumpkin** is an interactive browser mini-game where players nurture a pumpkin from seed to harvest using water, sunlight, and simple interactions.

Designed in Figma and developed using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, PixelPumpkin combines original pixel artwork, playful animations, and retro-inspired UI design.

## 🌱 Gameplay

Help your little pumpkin grow through three stages:

| Stage | Growth | Description |
|-------|--------|-------------|
| 🌰 Seed | 0% | Your pumpkin's journey begins! |
| 🌱 Sprout | 50% | Water your seed to help it sprout. |
| 🎃 Pumpkin | 100% | Give your sprout sunlight to finish growing! |

### Controls

- **WATER:** Water your seed and trigger animated droplets.
- **SUNLIGHT:** Give your sprout sunlight with glowing golden effects.
- **GROWTH:** Check your pumpkin's current progress.
- **RESTART:** Grow another pumpkin after reaching 100%.

## 🎨 Design Process

PixelPumpkin began as a UI/UX concept created in Figma.

The project evolved from static screen designs into an interactive prototype, then into a fully functional browser mini-game.

The goal was to explore how interface design, original artwork, animation, and JavaScript can work together to create a small but engaging digital experience.
